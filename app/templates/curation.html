How WikiHub curates

Anyone can publish on WikiHub, and nothing here is removed for being unhelpful. Instead, WikiHub's editors can record a judgement about a wiki, a single page or, rarely, a whole account. A judgement is an attributed, dated opinion with a reason, public evidence and the policy version it was made under. It can be replied to, corrected, retracted or left to expire, and it never deletes content.

What WikiHub does about it

While a "curated against" judgement is in force, WikiHub's own response (its enforcement) is:

  • a notice at the top of each page it covers, saying who made it, why and what it covers (readers can dismiss it);
  • the content stays readable by link but is left out of Explore, People, recent activity and its RSS feed, and llms.txt;
  • search engines are asked not to index it (noindex), and its outbound links are marked nofollow ugc;
  • it sorts last in WikiHub search.

Other services can read the judgements themselves at /api/v1/curation and decide how far to trust them. Pages also carry <meta name="wikihub:curation"> and API objects a curation field.

Reasons

promotional
Written mainly to promote a product or service, or to place links to it.
affiliate farm
Many near-identical pages built to send traffic to affiliate or partner links.
automated link placement
Links inserted by a bot or script rather than written for readers.
low quality
Little or no substance for a reader.
off topic
Content unrelated to what WikiHub is for.
featured
Recommended by WikiHub's editors; shown in Explore's Editorial Picks.
official
The official source for what it covers.
editor's pick
A wiki or page the editors want more people to find.

A recommendation never cancels a warning: if both apply, both are shown and the warning's effects stay.

Reply or appeal

If a notice is on your content and you think it is wrong, or the content has changed, use Send feedback in the menu and include the judgement number from the notice. An editor will review it and either retract the judgement, correct it, or link your published reply from the notice so readers see both sides. Every judgement also has a review date by which an editor looks at it again.

What curation is not

Curation is not removal and not access control. Content that exposes someone's personal data or carries a malicious payload is removed instead, by a separate action that leaves an audit record. Accounts that run link farms can be suspended.
