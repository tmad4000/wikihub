How WikiHub curates

Anyone can publish on WikiHub, and nothing here is removed for being unhelpful. Instead, WikiHub's editors can record a judgement about an account, a wiki or a page. Judgements are visible, reversible and never delete content.

"Curated against"

A page with this notice is still readable by link. WikiHub does not recommend it, and:

  • it is left out of Explore, People, recent activity and its RSS feed, and llms.txt;
  • search engines are asked not to index it, and its outbound links are marked nofollow ugc;
  • it sorts last in WikiHub search;
  • agents see it in <meta name="wikihub:curation"> and in the curation field of the API.
promotional
Written mainly to promote a product or service, or to place links to it.
affiliate farm
Many near-identical pages built to send traffic to affiliate or partner links.
automated link placement
Links inserted by a bot or script rather than written for readers.
low quality
Little or no substance for a reader.
off topic
Content unrelated to what WikiHub is for.

Recommended

The same mechanism works in the other direction: featured, official and editor's pick judgements put a wiki in Explore's editorial picks.

Disagree?

Judgements can be revoked. If you think a notice on your content is wrong, use Send feedback in the menu and say which page; an editor will look at it.
